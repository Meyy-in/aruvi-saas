Tour highlight — five options

Each option is shown on the four kinds of thing the tour points at: a lesson card, the + on a green section card, “Mark complete”, and a bottom-bar item. Light theme on the left, dark on the right. The highlight breathes the way it will on the phone. All five can be drawn on Android and iPhone the same way the current ring is.

A

Ochre ring

what you have now

Mustard-brown, borrowed from the web’s spotlight. It is the one colour on screen that belongs to nothing else in Meyy, which is why it reads as foreign. On green cards it goes muddy (3.1:1).

B

Pine ring

Meyy’s own green — the same pine as the tour box’s border and its Next button, so the ring and the box read as one thing. Strongest contrast of the coloured options in both themes (5.5:1 on green cards, 7.8:1 on white). The ring sits a few points outside the target, so it still shows around the pine “Mark complete” button.

C

Pine halo

The pine line plus a soft green wash just outside it, which breathes instead of the line. Gentler and more “lifted”, closest in feel to the website’s spotlight, without dimming anything. Takes a few more points of space around the target.

D

Clay ring

Terracotta — the colour the bottom bar already uses for “you are here” and the section card uses for a finished chapter. Warm and on-brand, but it shares meaning with “complete” on My Classes (4.7:1 on white, 3.3:1 on green).

E

Pine corner marks

Four pine corners instead of a full outline, like a camera viewfinder. The quietest and most editorial of the five; it frames the target without boxing it. Slightly less obvious on very small controls like the +.

Contrast figures are for the ring colour against the surface it sits on. 3:1 is the usual minimum for a non-text mark.
