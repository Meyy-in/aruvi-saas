Meyy is an online service. **Nothing is shipped** — there are no physical goods.

- **Delivery is immediate.** As soon as your payment is confirmed, the subject-stages you
  bought open in your Meyy account, on the mobile number you signed in with, on every device
  you use.
- Your invoice is sent by email when your account has an email address, and is always
  available in the app under **Settings › Subscription & billing**.
- If a payment has gone through but your subject does not open within a few minutes, write to
  [{{support_email}}](mailto:{{support_email}}) with your payment reference and we will put
  it right.

See also [Pricing](/pricing/) and [Refunds & cancellation](/refunds/).
