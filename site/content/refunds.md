## Cancellation

A purchase on Meyy is a **one-time payment for one year**. It does not renew automatically,
so there is no subscription to cancel, and you will not be charged again unless you choose to
buy again.

You can stop using Meyy at any time. You can also download everything Meyy holds about you,
and delete your account, from **Settings** in the app — whether or not you are subscribed.

## How a refund is paid

An approved refund is paid back to the original payment method, through Razorpay. Your bank
usually shows it within 5–7 working days of the refund being made.
