## What Meyy is

Meyy is a lesson-planning studio for teachers in Indian schools. Choose a subject, a class and
a chapter, and Meyy prepares the whole chapter as a lesson plan — period by period, with
timings, materials, homework and an assessment — aligned to NCF 2023 and fitted to the number
of periods you actually have.

Meyy covers Classes 3 to 9: Mathematics, Science, Social Science, English and The World Around
Us. It runs in your browser on a phone or a computer, on one account — your mobile number.

## Why it exists

Turning a textbook chapter into lessons that fit the week takes a teacher hours. Meyy does
that preparation in seconds, keeps track of where every section stopped, and leaves the
teacher's time for her students.
