## Before you subscribe — please read and confirm each of the following

*Meyy asks you to confirm these five points separately because they matter most to how you
will use the product. Ticking them is part of your agreement.*

---

### ☐ 1. Meyy is a teaching aid — it does not replace the textbook or you

Meyy is a supplementary lesson-planning and assessment tool. It does not replace the NCERT
(or state board) textbook, the prescribed syllabus, or your school's requirements. You should
continue to use the official textbook in your teaching. **You remain the professional
responsible for what is taught and assessed in your classroom.** Meyy's period
recommendations and annual budgets are Meyy's own estimates to help you plan — completing
the syllabus on your board's and school's schedule remains your responsibility.

**I understand and agree.**

---

### ☐ 2. Meyy is not endorsed by NCERT, CBSE, or any government authority

Meyy is an independent product. It is **not** endorsed, certified, approved by, or affiliated
with NCERT, CBSE, any state board, or the Ministry of Education. "NCF-aligned" and similar
statements describe **Meyy's own interpretation and design work** based on publicly available
framework documents — they are not an official certification. References to NCERT chapter
names, sections, and examples are for navigation and teaching support only; Meyy is not a
reproduction of, or substitute for, the NCERT textbook, which you should obtain separately.

**I understand and agree.**

---

### ☐ 3. Never enter students' personal information into Meyy

Meyy is built for **you**, not for student records. Do not enter any information that
identifies a student — names, roll numbers, marks, health or family details — into chapter
notes or any other input field. Meyy stores what you type on its servers, and
reserves the right to delete student-identifying information if it is inadvertently submitted.
This protects children under India's data-protection framework, and protects you.

**I understand and agree.**

---

### ☐ 4. Meyy's content is AI-assisted and may contain errors or change over time

Lesson plans and assessment items are authored with the help of AI models and reviewed
processes. They may contain factual, pedagogical, or answer-key errors, and as underlying
models and content libraries improve, outputs may change materially over time. Meyy's content
is an **assistive suggestion, not a guarantee** — review it with your own professional
judgement before teaching it. Meyy does not guarantee any particular examination result or
learning outcome.

**I understand and agree.**

---

### ☐ 5. Your licence is personal; the trial defines your remedy

Your subscription is a **personal licence to an individual teacher/user** for use in your own
teaching. You may not sell, redistribute, republish, or commercially exploit Meyy's plans,
exports, or other proprietary material. Your account is between you and Meyy — it is not
connected to your school or employer, and complying with your school's or organisation's
internal policies is your responsibility. Because Meyy strongly recommends you **use the
free trial before subscribing**
(the trial gives real chapters with unlimited re-serves and no time limit), subscription
refunds are considered **only for verified technical failures or billing errors** — not for
dissatisfaction with content you were able to evaluate in the trial.

**I understand and agree.**

# Full User Agreement & Information for Users (Draft outline)

*The sections below are the body of the full agreement the final tick accepts. Each expands
the five acknowledgements plus the remaining standard terms.*

## A. Who we are, and who you are
- Contracting entity: **Meyy (OPC) Private Limited**, India.
- The user: an **individual teacher/user** (teacher, trainer, home-schooler, or other
  individual) subscribing in a personal capacity. Meyy does not
  currently offer school/enterprise accounts; if it does in future, those will be governed by
  separate terms or an addendum — nothing in this agreement binds your employer, and nothing
  your employer does binds this agreement.

## B. The service
- What Meyy does: NCF-informed lesson planning, period allocation, assessment items,
  chapter notes, progress tracking, exports (PDF/DOCX).
- Meyy's recommendations on the length of lesson plans are based on its own algorithm;
  you are free to use what you think is appropriate.
- Meyy is under active development; features may change, be added, or be withdrawn. No
  uptime or availability guarantee (no SLA) is offered at this stage. You can export your
  data at any time (Section G).

## C. Trial, subscription, and billing
- **Trial is strongly suggested before subscribing** and is the best way to evaluate Meyy:
  all subject-stages, any 3 chapters, unlimited re-serves per chapter, no time limit.
- **The free trial is once per mobile number.** Deleting your account and signing up again
  does not start a new trial: the trial chapters already used on that number are still counted
  for 24 months after the deletion (see §G and the Privacy Notice §7).
- Billing unit: per teacher/user, per **subject-stage** (e.g. Science–Middle), unlimited plan
  serves within that scope.
- On subscription lapse: your prepared plans remain accessible; the progress tracker is
  suspended until renewal. Trial-exhausted accounts keep plans and tracker.
- Pricing may change with prior notice; changes never apply retroactively to a paid term.

## D. Refunds
- Refunds are considered for **verified technical failure** (persistent inability to access
  paid features attributable to Meyy) and **billing errors** (e.g. double charge).
- Subjective dissatisfaction with content or pedagogy is not a refund ground, given the
  unrestricted free trial. Refund requests: within **1 week**, with proof of the technical
  interruption, to **[support email]**.

## E. Intellectual property
- Meyy's engine, libraries, plans, mappings, and design are Meyy's proprietary material.
- You receive a personal, non-transferable licence to use plans and exports in your own
  teaching. No resale, redistribution, or republication.
- Your own chapter notes are yours; you grant Meyy the licence needed to
  store and display them back to you.
- NCERT/framework references are used for educational navigation under India's fair-dealing
  principles.
- If you paste third-party content into Meyy, you are responsible for having the right to
  use it (indemnity).

## F. Data protection (DPDP Act, 2023) — current compliance status
- What Meyy collects: your user ID/account details, teaching profile (subjects, classes,
  sections as labels like "9A"), plans, notes, and usage needed to run the service.
- What Meyy does **not** collect: student personal data (and you agree not to enter any —
  Acknowledgement 3).
- Processors: content generation uses third-party AI providers; payments will use a payment
  gateway when live. Your notes and profile are not used to train third-party models
  **[verify with provider terms]**.
- **Compliance status stated honestly:** Meyy is implementing DPDP compliance in stages.
  Data export and erasure are live today (Section G). A grievance officer has not yet been
  appointed; until then, all data-protection requests and complaints go to
  **[founder contact email]**, and this document will be updated when the appointment is made.
- Retention: account data kept while the account exists; erased on request (Section G).

## G. Your data rights (live today)
- **Export:** download everything Meyy holds about you as a single editable document,
  from Settings.
- **Erase:** delete your account and all personal state with typed confirmation; you receive
  an erasure receipt stating exactly what was removed and what was kept (shared, non-personal
  content libraries are not personal data and are retained).
- **What survives an erase, and why:** the record that you accepted this agreement — its
  version, the date, and which points you confirmed — is kept after your account is deleted,
  as evidence of the agreement itself. It holds no lesson plans, notes, teaching profile or
  school details. Statutory tax records for payments you made are kept for the period the law
  requires. So that the free trial stays once per number, a keyed one-way code made from your
  mobile number and the number of trial chapters it used are kept for 24 months — never the
  number itself, and nothing else. Everything else personal to you is removed, and backups are
  purged within 30 days.
- **A kept record is not a continuing agreement.** Your acceptance stands from the day you
  give it until your account is erased — across sign-outs and sessions, and whether or not
  you go on to subscribe. Erasing your account ends it. If you use Meyy again afterwards,
  you will be asked to read and accept the agreement afresh; the kept record remains only as
  evidence of what you agreed to before.

## H. Acceptable use
- One account per teacher/user; keep your credentials to yourself.
- No scraping, bulk extraction, reverse engineering, or use of Meyy to build a competing
  dataset or service.
- No unlawful content in notes or inputs.

  consequential loss, including examination or career outcomes.
- Nothing limits liability that cannot be limited under law, including under the Consumer
  Protection Act, 2019.

## J. Changes, governing law, disputes
- Meyy may update this agreement; material changes are notified in-app and require

- Governing law: India. Jurisdiction: **Chennai, Tamil Nadu**.

## K. Marketing emails (optional)
- The optional box at the end of this agreement lets Meyy email you occasionally about new
  subjects, features and teaching resources. It is genuinely optional: leaving it unticked
  does not affect your trial, your subscription, or anything you can do in Meyy.
- **Service emails are not marketing and are not covered by this choice.** Meyy will still

  changes to this agreement or your subscription, and anything required for security or by law.
- **Email only.** Meyy will not send marketing by SMS, WhatsApp or phone call, even though

- **Withdraw at any time** — in Settings, or from the unsubscribe link in any marketing
  email. It takes effect immediately, needs no reason, and is as easy as giving it was.
- Your choice is stored with your account, appears in your data export, and is deleted when
  you erase your account.
