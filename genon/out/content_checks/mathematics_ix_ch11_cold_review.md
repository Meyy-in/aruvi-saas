## Summary

42 findings in total: **S1: 1** (#1, P11 Item 13, which marks the correct count of 50 reductions for gcd(97,2) as "48"). **S2: 14** (#2–#15): 12 in the canonical plan, 1 in P11 and 1 in P08. **S3: 27** (#16–#42). Of the S3s, 2 are book issues (#16, #17), 3 are marked unsure (#30, #36, #37), 1 is a coverage note (#42) and 2 are register-only rows (#31, #32). The S2s are mostly muddled reasoning in marking criteria and guides: why the refinements are correct, the tiling biconditional, how division saves work, the lcm item's false premise, and the forward vs backward scan comparison. There is also one paraphrase of a book algorithm with the wrong step count (P08 Item 7) and one piece of leftover drafting (P11 Unit 10). All MCQ keys, all NUM answers, every divisor list and every book page, exercise and question reference are correct.

Not verifiable or judgement calls: whether the canonical Unit 14 consolidation (#2) should count as S1 (it is wrong only in the m = n edge case and on per-step cost); whether C-7.1 (#36) is a legitimate cross-grade competency code; whether the "about 49" approximations (#30) need fixing at all; and the historical claim in #37, which goes beyond the book (no outside sources were consulted). The starred items in #42 may be left out on purpose.
